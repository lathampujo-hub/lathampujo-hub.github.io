Bonzai Buddy privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Bonzai Buddy is a personal app that runs on its owner's Mac. Only its owner signs in to it with Google.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads and changes the owner's Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks, contacts and Apps Script projects, only when the owner asks it to.

Where data goes

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac.
  • There is no Bonzai Buddy server. Google data is not collected, sold or shared with anyone.
  • When the owner asks the app's AI assistant about something, the relevant text is sent to the AI service the owner has chosen, to answer that request.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
